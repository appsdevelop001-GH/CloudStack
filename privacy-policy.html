Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026  |  Last Updated: November 25, 2026

Welcome to CloudStack ("the Game", "we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to keeping your gaming experience safe, transparent, and enjoyable. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Game stores and how it is used.

Quick Summary: CloudStack does not collect personal identity details (such as real name, phone number, email address, contacts, photos, or GPS location). Your avatar (DP), game settings, and level progress are stored locally on your device.

1. Information We Store

  • Player Nickname / Game ID & High Score: When you create an in-game nickname (or play as a Guest) and complete levels, your chosen Game ID and highest score may be submitted to our global leaderboard service so your rank can be displayed inside the 3D Hexagon Rank Tower.
  • Local Device Storage (localStorage): Your selected Avatar (DP emoji/icon), unlocked levels, best scores, audio/vibration preferences, and gameplay statistics are saved locally on your device.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To save your game progress, unlocked levels (Levels 1–12 and Infinity Mode), and personal best score across sessions.
  • To display your Game ID, chosen Avatar (DP), and rank position in the in-game 3D Hexagon Leaderboard & Live Challenge mode.
  • To remember your audio, haptic vibration, reduced motion, and graphics quality preferences.

3. Data Reset & User Control

You have full control over your data at any time. Inside the Game, open Settings → Reset Game and confirm to immediately erase your saved Game ID, Avatar (DP), best scores, unlocked levels, and local statistics from your device.

4. Permissions & Third-Party Sharing

  • We do not sell, trade, or rent any player data to third parties.
  • The Game only uses standard internet access to fetch and update leaderboard scores and load this Privacy Policy page inside the in-game web preview.
  • The Game does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or precise location.

5. Children's Privacy

CloudStack is a family-friendly arcade game suitable for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Players are encouraged to use a fun, non-personal game nickname.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as we add new features. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy or CloudStack, please reach out through our official developer support page or repository.
